I've decided to keep a running notebook here: longer pieces when I have
something worked out, and shorter notes linking to things I've been reading
and thinking about.

Most of my work sits at the seam between social science research and the
practical business of building data teams inside mission-driven organizations.
That's the ground I expect to cover most often here ---methods, research
infrastructure, and how institutions actually put evidence to use.
